Privacy Policy

Mattress by Appointment Wausau

Effective date: August 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how SLH Global Ventures LLC, doing business as Mattress by Appointment Wausau (“we,” “us,” or “our”), an independently owned and operated mattress retailer in Wausau, Wisconsin, collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit www.mattressbyappointmentwisconsin.com, interact with our ads on Facebook, Instagram, or other Meta platforms, message us, call or text us, submit our appointment request form, opt in to our text messages, or visit us for an appointment.

We run advertising on Meta platforms (including Facebook and Instagram). Meta requires that we publish a privacy policy explaining how advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel collect and share data. This policy is written for that purpose and for our customers in Wisconsin and anywhere else our ads may be shown.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

We do not currently operate an online checkout or account on this website. You may give us information when you:

  • Submit our online appointment request form (name, phone number, email, and the details you choose to share)
  • Check a box to opt in to text messages from us

You may also give us information when you:

  • Call or text us at 715-280-6800
  • Message us on Facebook Messenger
  • Schedule or attend a private appointment
  • Purchase a mattress, adjustable base, or related product in person
  • Ask about payment plans or financing

That information may include your name, phone number, message content, mattress size or preferences, appointment date and time, purchase details, and, if you apply for financing, information needed by a financing partner (which is collected and processed by that partner under its own privacy policy).

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website or interact with our ads, we and our advertising partners automatically collect:

  • Device and browser information (such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and language)
  • Pages viewed, links clicked (including Call and Message buttons), dates and times of visits, and referring URLs
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Cookie identifiers, pixel IDs, and similar online identifiers
  • Information about how you arrived at our site from an advertisement (including Meta ad click IDs)

3. Cookies, pixels, and the Meta Pixel

We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate our site, measure traffic, and run and measure advertising.

Meta Pixel. We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel) on this website. The Pixel is a snippet of JavaScript provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. It collects information about your visit — including pages viewed, buttons clicked (such as Call or Message Us), your IP address, browser and device data, and cookie identifiers — and sends that information to Meta. If you are logged into Facebook or Instagram, Meta may associate that activity with your account.

We use this data so we can:

  • Show you relevant ads on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and other Meta placements
  • Measure whether our ads lead to website visits, calls, messages, and other actions
  • Build custom audiences and lookalike audiences
  • Limit how often you see our ads and improve campaign performance

Meta may also use this information to improve its own advertising and measurement services, as described in Meta’s Privacy Policy and Meta’s explanation of Business Tools.

We may also use Meta’s Conversions API or similar tools that send website event data (such as a page view or form submission) to Meta to improve ad measurement. We do not send phone numbers or text messaging consent to Meta or any other advertising platform.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to calls, texts, and Messenger conversations and schedule appointments
  • Complete in-store sales and provide customer service
  • Send text messages only to people who have opted in, as described in Section 5A
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Run, target, personalize, and measure advertisements, including on Meta platforms
  • Create and use website-visitor audiences, lookalike audiences, and retargeting campaigns
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and technical issues
  • Comply with law and enforce our rights

5. Advertising and Meta (Facebook & Instagram)

We advertise on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and the Meta Audience Network. Those ads may be shown to people in Wisconsin and surrounding areas who may be interested in mattresses, home furnishings, or similar products, and to people who have visited our website or contacted us.

In connection with those ads, we may share information with Meta, or Meta may collect information through our Pixel and ads, including:

  • Website events, such as page views, contact button clicks, and form submissions
  • The fact that you clicked a Call or Message Us button
  • Information needed to measure ad performance and optimize delivery

If you message us through Facebook Messenger, Meta processes that conversation under its own terms. We use the content of those messages to answer you and schedule your appointment.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do allow Meta to collect online identifiers (such as cookie IDs) and website activity through the Meta Pixel so we can show and measure ads. This never includes your phone number or text messaging consent. Some privacy laws treat that kind of sharing as a “sale” or “share” for cross-context behavioral advertising. See Section 8 for your choices.

5A. Text messaging (SMS) and mobile information

If you check a box on our appointment request form to receive text messages, we collect your mobile phone number, the date and time of your consent, and which types of messages you agreed to. We use that information only to send the messages you asked for:

  • Appointment and customer care messages, such as appointment confirmations, reminders, scheduling updates, and replies to your questions
  • Marketing messages (only if you checked the marketing box), such as special offers, discounts, sales, new inventory, and service updates

Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also call 715-280-6800. Full program terms are in our Terms & Conditions.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or text messaging consent with anyone for their own marketing, and we do not buy or sell leads. The sharing described elsewhere in this policy (including with Meta) does not apply to mobile phone numbers or consent collected through our SMS opt-in. The only providers that handle this information are the messaging platform and phone carriers that deliver our texts, and they may use it only to provide that service to us.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to run the business. None of the sharing below includes text messaging opt-in data or consent, except delivery of our texts by our messaging provider as described in Section 5A.

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — advertising, measurement, Messenger, and the Meta Pixel, as described above
  • Website hosting and technical providers — to serve and secure this website
  • Communications tools — phone, text messaging, form, and CRM services we use to receive your requests and reply to you, acting only on our behalf
  • Financing or payment partners — if you choose a payment plan in store; those partners receive the information they need to process your application and collect payment, under their own policies
  • Professional advisors and service providers — such as accountants or IT support, under appropriate confidentiality
  • Authorities — when required by law, or to protect our customers, our store, or our rights
  • A buyer of the business — if we sell or transfer the store, customer purchase and appointment records (not text messaging opt-in data or consent) may transfer as part of that transaction

We do not sell, rent, or trade customer lists or phone numbers to anyone.

7. Your choices

Ads on Facebook and Instagram

You can review and change your ad preferences in your Meta account:

  • Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Instagram privacy and security settings
  • Your Facebook ad settings, including whether ads are shown based on activity from partners

You can also use Meta’s tools to opt out of seeing ads from this business, or to hide our ads. Opting out of personalized ads does not stop all ads — you may still see ads that are not based on your profile.

Cookies and industry opt-outs

  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies may limit the Pixel and some site features.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: optout.aboutads.info
  • Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org
  • If you use a mobile device, you can limit ad tracking in iOS or Android settings.

Calls, texts, and Messenger

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can block or hide our Messenger thread at any time. We still need to keep records of purchases and appointments as required for the business.

8. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, and other states with consumer privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we have about you
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, or of targeted advertising
  • Appeal a decision we make about a privacy request

We do not use sensitive personal information from this website to infer characteristics about you. We do not knowingly “sell” or “share” the personal information of consumers under 16.

To exercise these rights, call or text us at 715-280-6800 and ask for a privacy request. We will need to verify who you are (for example, by matching the phone number you used to contact us). You may use an authorized agent where the law allows it; we may still need to verify the request with you directly.

Do not sell or share / opt out of targeted advertising. You can make that request by calling or texting 715-280-6800. You can also limit Meta’s use of your data through the ad settings linked in Section 7. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising where required by law.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

9. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and customer records as long as needed to schedule appointments, complete sales, provide service, measure advertising, and meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements. Website analytics and ad-platform data are kept according to those platforms’ retention settings and our business needs. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including limiting who can access customer records. No website, ad platform, or phone system is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive financial information (such as full Social Security numbers or bank details) by unsecured text or Messenger; financing applications are handled in person or through the financing partner’s process.

11. Children

Our website and ads are intended for adults shopping for mattresses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not direct our services to children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, call us and we will delete it.

12. Third-party sites and services

Our “Message Us” button opens Facebook Messenger, which is operated by Meta. Our ads appear on Meta’s apps and websites. Those services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for Meta’s practices. Please read Meta’s policies before using Messenger or a Facebook/Instagram account.
